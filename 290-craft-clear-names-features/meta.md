# Session 290 — Craft clear names for features and labels in your app

- **URL:** https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/290/
- **Duration:** 15m
- **Speakers:** Heej (UX writer, Human Interface design team)

## Description

The names you give things — from hero features down to menus, settings, and tab
bars — shape how people experience your app, yet they're easy to overlook. This
session gives you three tools to make naming choices well: a set of **criteria**
for what makes a name work, a **process** for generating candidate names from
your audience's perspective, and a way to **evaluate** the names you come up with.
It's a UX-writing / design talk — no code, no APIs.

## Key topics

- **Three criteria** for a good name: it **belongs** (fits the app and the rest
  of what you've named), it **sets the right expectation** (clarity + trust), and
  it **works everywhere** (across languages, markets, platforms, and contexts).
- Criteria are a **guide, not a rulebook** — you won't always check every box, and
  you may add your own (trademark, industry regulation). The trade-offs are yours.
- **Clarity vs. brand** tension — sometimes the obvious word (Balance) is right;
  sometimes a branded name (Lightweight/Heavyweight) fits but costs comprehension.
- **The "think / feel / do" exercise** — start from the audience, brainstorm one
  idea per sticky note without filtering, group by theme, then gut-check names by
  dropping them into natural sentences ("Hey, check out ___").
- **Naming styles** all judged by the same criteria: descriptive (Enhance Dialogue),
  emotional (Memories), and branded/coined (AutoMix).
- **Verbs for actions** — name a feature you *do* with a verb to put the user in
  control (Enhance Dialogue, not Vocal Isolation).
- Good names **compound** — each one makes the next easier and becomes the app's language.

## Apple examples used

- **Apple Cash** — labeling the available amount: Spending Power ✗ → Current Funds ✗ → **Balance** ✓
- **Gym app** (hypothetical) — Basic/All Access vs. Lightweight/Heavyweight (clarity vs. brand)
- **Apple Maps** — the "think/feel/do" walkthrough → **Visited Places** (rejecting "Private Memories")
- **Apple Photos** — **Memories** (emotional clarity over a technical "algorithmic photo grouping" label)
- **Apple Podcasts** — Vocal Isolation / Isolate Vocals / Clarify Speech / Enhance Playback → **Enhance Dialogue** (also on Apple TV)
- **Apple Music** — **AutoMix** (coined word that explains itself from its parts)
