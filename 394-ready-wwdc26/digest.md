# WWDC26 · 394 — Get ready for WWDC26

## TL;DR

A ~2-minute hype-and-logistics teaser. No technical content — it tells developers
how to prepare for WWDC26:

1. **Get the Apple Developer app** — the hub for experiencing the whole conference (and new stickers, including the dogcow Clarus).
2. **Create a free developer account** — unlocks personalized recommendations across **100+ sessions** on developer.apple.com.
3. **Set reminders** — **Keynote Monday, June 8 at 10 a.m. Pacific**; **Platforms State of the Union at 1 p.m.**
4. **Sign up for Group Labs** — live-streaming Q&A with the Apple engineers and designers behind the technologies.
5. **Use the Developer Forums** and **find your community** — activities run all week, online and worldwide.

WWDC26 takes place **June 8–12, 2026**, all online and free.
