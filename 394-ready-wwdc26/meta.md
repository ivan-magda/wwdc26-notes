## Description

A short promotional roundup that previews WWDC26 and tells developers how to prepare:
download the Apple Developer app, set up a free developer account for personalized
session recommendations, set reminders for the Keynote and Platforms State of the
Union, sign up for Group Labs, use the Developer Forums, and join community activities.
WWDC26 runs June 8–12, 2026, all online and free.

## Key topics

- **Apple Developer app** — primary way to experience the conference; new stickers including the dogcow Clarus
- **Free Apple Developer account** — unlocks personalized recommendations across 100+ sessions on developer.apple.com
- **Schedule** — Keynote Monday, June 8 at 10 a.m. Pacific; Platforms State of the Union follows at 1 p.m.
- **Group Labs** — live-streaming Q&A with Apple engineers and designers
- **Developer Forums** — get answers now and during WWDC
- **Community** — activities all week, online and around the world
- **Extras** — Apple Music WWDC playlist, 2026 Apple Design Award finalists, WWDC26 wallpaper, Alien/Clarus stickers
