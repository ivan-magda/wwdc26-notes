**Format:** WWDC26 Keynote, 01:16:02, machine transcript via whisper (whisper.cpp ggml-large-v3 from keynote audio, no speaker diarization).

## Description

The ~76-minute WWDC26 Keynote, recorded at Apple Park. A fast-paced, multi-presenter
product and platform announcement organized around three stated themes for this year's
OS releases: (1) responsiveness and ease-of-use refinements, (2) trust and safety
(especially for kids), and (3) "a big leap forward for Apple Intelligence" with a new
architecture and an all-new Siri. The headline news is the next generation of Apple
Intelligence, built in collaboration with Google's Gemini technology, and "Siri AI."
The naming year confirms iOS 27 and macOS GoldenGate. No new hardware was announced in
this keynote; it is software-only. It closes with Tim Cook and an App Store developer
rap montage.

## Major segments / announcements (in order)

1. **Open / welcome** — App Store scale ("well over 1,000 submissions every hour"),
   20 Apple Developer Academies, framing of the day.
2. **Three focus areas** stated up front: responsiveness/ease-of-use, trust & safety
   for kids, Apple Intelligence + new Siri. Releases span iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, tvOS,
   visionOS, macOS.
3. **macOS naming gag** — next macOS is **macOS GoldenGate**.
4. **Refinements / "sweating the details"**
   - **Design:** Liquid Glass refinements; new clarity-to-tint slider; macOS toolbar/
     sidebar changes; app icons with embedded Liquid Glass layers.
   - **Performance:** app launch up to 30% faster; new photos appear up to 70% faster;
     AirDrop up to 80% faster; iPad-to-external-drive up to 5x faster; CPU scheduler
     improvements brought back to **iPhone 11**; **iOS 27** supports the same models as
     iOS 26.
   - **Connectivity, messaging send indicator, rebuilt search index** (Spotlight,
     Photos, Mail), new Mail ranking.
   - **App features grab-bag:** Shared Albums (Android/Windows contributors, full-res),
     Health Cycle Tracking perimenopause/menopause, AirPods custom EQ, Vision Pro
     panoramas-to-spatial-scenes, Maps Flyover boost.
5. **Trust & safety for kids** — child accounts; content controls; Ask to Browse (web
   approval, joins Ask to Buy); communication limits / contact approval; communication
   safety extended to gore/violence; time allowances by category; schedules;
   redesigned Screen Time; developer APIs (Declared Age Range API).
6. **Apple Intelligence — new architecture** — Apple Foundation Models built with
   Google Gemini technology; on-device + server (Private Cloud Compute); a second,
   more powerful on-device model for the most capable Apple Silicon; system
   orchestrator; personal context, App Actions / app toolbox, on-screen awareness,
   broad world knowledge.
7. **Siri AI** — rebuilt Siri; conversational; dedicated Siri app (iCloud-synced);
   expressive customizable voice; improved dictation; across iOS, iPadOS, macOS
   (Spotlight integration), watchOS, visionOS, CarPlay, AirPods.
8. **Visual intelligence + writing** — camera Siri mode; macOS keyboard shortcut; iPad
   screenshot integration; visionOS look-to-ask; writing with Siri anywhere;
   system-wide automatic proofreading.
9. **Apple Intelligence across apps** — Safari (tame tabs, Notify Me, Describe an
   Extension), Passwords (agentic password updates), Messages/Mail/Calendar/Phone
   (call context, on-device), Home (camera clip summaries, 4K), Shortcuts (describe a
   shortcut), Image Playground (new generative model, photorealistic), Photos (cleanup,
   extend, **spatial reframing**).
10. **Availability & limits** — free with latest releases; daily limits on server
    features; iCloud+ for increased access; **Siri AI English-first, beta later this
    year**; **not initially in EU (iOS/iPadOS)**; **not in China** pending regulation.
11. **For developers** — App Intents → Siri; Foundation Models framework (image input,
    custom tools/skills, server models via same Swift API); new **Core AI** framework
    (run other models locally); **Xcode** coding assistant (localize whole app, drive
    simulated devices, custom skills, model/agent choice incl. **Gemini**, Figma/GitHub
    connectors); **Device Hub**.
12. **Close** — Tim Cook; betas today, public beta next month, GA this fall; App Store
    developer rap montage.
