# WWDC26 · 121 — Announcing Apple's next big step for Siri and iPhone

## TL;DR

This is **not a technical session** — it's a ~1-minute **cinematic "movie trailer"
teaser** that hypes the year's headline consumer features for Siri and iPhone. Treat
it as a keynote sizzle reel, not a developer talk. The themes it advertises:

1. **Siri AI as a personal assistant** — a "glow-up," ask it anything, and a **new
   dedicated app** to revisit past conversations.
2. **Siri running multi-step tasks** — meal nutrition lookups, coordinating a soccer
   watch party. The marketing framing: "main character energy," "puts the personal in
   personal assistant."
3. **Next-generation Apple Intelligence in Photos** — extend, reframe, and clean up.
4. **Image Playground** — photorealistic image generation for Contact Posters and
   wallpapers.
5. **Safari** — organize by topics, plus **Notify Me**.
6. **Passwords** — compromised passwords auto-updated "with just a tap."
7. **Privacy** — Apple Intelligence as a "sentinel" keeping private data private.

## 1. Cold open — Siri's "glow-up"

The teaser opens in trailer-voiceover style ("Coming to a pocket near you!") with
dramatic music and a single Siri line: "Hello." It bills **Siri AI** in "the role of a
lifetime: your personal assistant" — you can **ask Siri AI anything** and **revisit
your conversations** in a **new dedicated app**.

## 2. Siri runs tasks ("a quest")

Two example tasks dramatize Siri handling end-to-end detail:

- Providing **nutritional details about your meal**.
- Handling **every detail of a soccer watch party**.

Marketing pull-quotes: "Siri is giving main character energy" and "puts the personal
in personal assistant."

## 3. Next-generation Apple Intelligence — Photos

Apple Intelligence can **extend, reframe, and clean up** your photos.

## 4. Image Playground

Create **photorealistic images** and use them "everywhere" — from **Contact Posters**
to **wallpapers** — in the **all-new Image Playground**.

## 5. Safari

- Organize with **topics**.
- Stay timely with **Notify Me**.

## 6. Passwords

**Compromised passwords are automatically updated with just a tap.**

## 7. Privacy

Apple Intelligence "stands guard" as a "faithful sentinel," keeping private info
private — "your data stays protected." The teaser closes: "All this and so much more.
Coming to an iPhone near you. … Sweet."
