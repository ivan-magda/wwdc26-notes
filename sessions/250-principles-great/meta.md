# Session 250 — Principles of great design

- **URL:** https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/250/
- **Duration:** 17m
- **Speakers:** Linda & Doug (Design Evangelists at Apple)

## Description

A foundational, non-technical talk on what "great design" means on Apple platforms.
Design is framed not as how something looks or behaves, but as *making something with
intention* — focusing on what matters most to people so you build something they truly
value. The session walks through eight design principles, stressing that there's no
formula for combining them and that leaning into one can mean compromising another.

## Key topics

- **Purpose** — design experiences that genuinely serve and adapt to people; decide what *not* to build before writing code
- **Agency** — put people in control via choices, autonomy, and forgiveness (easy undo, confirmation on destructive actions, sparing interruptions)
- **Responsibility** — privacy as a human right, ask only for what's necessary with context, anticipate harm (esp. AI), remove features when risk > value
- **Familiarity** — lean on real-world and interface conventions; metaphors that aren't too literal or abstract; consistency in behavior and placement
- **Flexibility** — adapt to contexts, devices, and abilities; design to each platform's strengths; let people personalize when no single solution fits
- **Simplicity** — not minimalism but removing friction; concise language, strong hierarchy, clear affordances; sometimes adding context makes things simpler
- **Craft** — uncompromising attention to detail (typography, color, animation, performance) that inspires trust; comes from iteration and ongoing maintenance
- **Delight** — not confetti; the natural result of getting everything else right; identify the emotion you want people to feel and reinforce it

## Chapter summary (Summary tab)

- **0:00 Intro** — Design is more than aesthetics or behavior; it's making something with intention. Every feature asks for a person's time, attention, and trust, so choosing what *not* to build matters as much as what you do build.
- **1:08 Purpose** — Design experiences that genuinely serve people: respect and adapt to their lives, are clear and considered, and at their best a true joy to use. Before writing code, ask whether what you're making has real purpose and delivers real value.
- **1:52 Agency** — Put people in control. Let them explore at their own pace, avoid forcing predetermined paths, and build in forgiveness: easy undo and confirmation for destructive actions, so they feel confident and free to try things.
- **3:38 Responsibility** — Respect privacy as a human right, ask only for what's necessary, and anticipate how features could cause harm. Safeguard people from bad outcomes, especially with AI, and remove features when risks outweigh value.
- **6:04 Familiarity** — Let people apply existing knowledge to your design. Use metaphors that aren't too literal or abstract, follow established conventions for common actions, and keep behavior consistent: things that look the same should work the same.
- **8:52 Flexibility** — Adapt to the many different contexts, devices, and abilities of users. Design for each platform's strengths, consider the full range of your audience, and let people personalize when no single solution fits everyone.
- **11:13 Simplicity** — Simplicity isn't minimalism; it's removing friction. Concise language, strong visual hierarchy, and clear affordances help people find what they need without effort. Every element should earn its place, and sometimes adding context makes an interface simpler.
- **13:42 Craft** — The uncompromising attention to detail that builds trust. Beautiful typography, responsive animations, solid performance, and a commitment to iteration show people you care. Great design has longevity — keep evolving it as platforms and needs change.
- **15:47 Delight** — Delight isn't confetti; it's the natural result of getting everything else right. Identify the emotion you want people to feel, then reinforce it through your design. Combine all the principles with genuine care and you create something people truly love.
