## Description

The day-two episode of WWDC26's daily recap show. Host Lauren Grimm points
viewers to on-demand Keynote and Platform State of the Union replays (plus a SOTU
recap video and takeaways article), then hands off to a sit-down interview where
Jeff talks with Josh Schaffer — who MC'd the Platform State of the Union — about
the year's three themes and what the team is most proud of. Closes with the day's
logistics: Group Labs running all day, AI-powered answers on developer.apple.com,
Apple Music WWDC26 playlists, and the 100+ sessions that dropped on day one.

## Key topics

- The three SOTU themes of the year: **rebuilt intelligence**, **refined platforms**, **transformed tools**
- "Transformed tools" = Xcode's shift toward **agentic coding**; download Xcode and try the new agent capabilities
- Intelligence at two layers: **in apps** (more powerful, new capabilities) and **at the system level** (Siri, system-wide interactions) — described as complementary
- Schaffer's "proudest, less obvious" pick: **Foundation Models framework** access to a **frontier model in Private Cloud Compute** with no business-model/token-billing/server-wiring setup — framed as democratizing frontier-model access
- 100+ sessions spanning Core AI, Xcode 27, design foundations — broad-interest down to niche, all streaming now
- Logistics: **Group Labs** all day (live presentations + Q&A with Apple engineers/designers); AI-powered answers via the developer.apple.com search bar; Apple Music "WWDC26" playlists
